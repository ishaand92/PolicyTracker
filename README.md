# 🌍 PolicyTracker – Climate Policy Impact Dashboard
**Website:** [policytracker.app](http://policytracker.app)

## 🧠 Project Summary
PolicyTracker is a data-driven platform that helps users understand and compare the effectiveness of proposed climate policies. By leveraging historical climate data and predictive modeling, the site presents policy outcomes in a clear and interactive format. Key metrics—such as greenhouse gas reductions, renewable energy adoption, and policy costs—are visualized to empower voters, researchers, and students with actionable insight.

## 🎯 Purpose
The project was created to fill a gap in public understanding of climate legislation. PolicyTracker transforms complex policy documents into digestible, visual comparisons, promoting transparency and encouraging informed civic engagement.
