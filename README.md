# PolicyTracker (policytracker.app)

## ✅ Capstone Project Type
> **Type Selected:**  
> ✅ Innovative Practical Project

## 📝 Project Summary
This project aims to evaluate the effectiveness of proposed climate legislation and present it to voters. It will use past trends and data relating to climate policies and compare them to a currently proposed policy. The project would present the policy based on specific metrics, such as greenhouse gas emission reductions or percentage of renewable energy. The project would inform voters more about the climate policies that are currently being proposed and make information relating to a policy’s impact more accessible.

## 💡 Novelty Justification
This project addresses a significant gap in accessible information about climate policies and their potential impacts. Currently, there are limited platforms that provide citizens with comprehensive, user-friendly evaluations of proposed environmental legislation, making this a novel and valuable resource for informed decision-making.

## 👥 Community Benefit
The project will benefit voters and citizens by providing them with transparent, accessible, and actionable information about climate policies. This increased access to reliable data can inform voting decisions and promote greater civic engagement on climate-related issues.

## 🔗 Interdisciplinary Nature
This project combines both data science and analysis, as that is how the information will be compiled, with climate change, as the project will be analyzing data relating to environmental policies and climate action.

## 📊 Measuring Impact
- Number of policies evaluated.
- Website traffic and user engagement.
- Survey-based improvements in user understanding and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 SMART Project Goals

1. **Policy Assessments**: Evaluate the effectiveness of over 50 climate policies within the first month of launch.  
2. **User Engagement**: Achieve at least 1,000 unique site visitors within the first three months.  
3. **Data Presentation**: Provide detailed visualizations for at least five key metrics (e.g., GHG reductions, renewable energy adoption) for each policy.  
4. **User Feedback**: Collect feedback from at least 200 users through surveys to measure changes in understanding and decision-making within six months.  
5. **Community Impact**: Facilitate at least 10 collaborations with environmental organizations or voter education groups within the first year.
